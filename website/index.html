01 / Choose your starting point

Two modes.
One local workflow.

Start with the condition of your recording. Listen to the export before using it; results depend on the source.

01CLEAN + LEVEL

Raw

For speech that needs cleaning as well as leveling. The Original preset combines declipping, a high-pass filter, declicking, denoising, gating and loudness processing.

Cleaning and leveling↗
02LEVEL

Zoom / Teams

For recordings that already have call-app noise processing. This mode uses dynamic leveling and loudness processing without Raw's cleaning stages.

Leveling only↗
*

Keep a listening check in your workflow. Fast is the default and does not measure final loudness. Accurate is an optional measured workflow that takes longer and can sound different. Gentle is an experimental Raw preset without speech listening approval.

Your recording stays with you

Select. Process.
Review. Listen.

No account or upload is needed. Processing runs through the existing BAT or PowerShell entry point. The website presents the tool; it is not required to run it.

  1. 01

    Select your source

    Use a readable local mono/stereo recording and choose Raw or Zoom.

  2. 02

    Keep a separate export

    The tool validates and publishes a new WAV without replacing your source or earlier exports.

  3. 03

    Read the result, then listen

    Review the console and available reports. Valid audio with warnings still needs your attention.

02 / Get started

A small tool.
A clear setup.

Use your normal Windows account and keep the complete extracted package together in a short, writable folder.

Windows
Windows 10 / 11 are intended targets. Other builds and managed security configurations are not universally verified.
PowerShell
Windows PowerShell 5.1 or PowerShell 7. The BAT uses 5.1 by default.
FFmpeg + ffprobe
Supply both Windows executables from a trusted build. They are separate dependencies and are not bundled or downloaded automatically.
